# Alphabet and sounds

Swedish uses the same 26 letters as English **plus three more**: `å, ä, ö`. They come at the end — 29 letters total.

- `å` like “o” in “more”
- `ä` like “e” in “bed”
- `ö` like “u” in “fur” (no r)

These are approximations — listen to a native speaker when you can.

## Why it matters

One dot changes meaning: `har` (have) vs `här` (here). Spelling counts.

## Practice

- `Hej!` (Hello!)
- `Tack!` (Thanks!)
- `Fika` — the Swedish coffee break with something sweet.
