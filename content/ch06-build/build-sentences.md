# Build sentences

Follow this checklist each time:

1. Pick the subject (`jag`, `du`, `hon`…).
2. Pick the verb. Present (`äter`), or infinitive (`äta`) after `vill`/`kan`/`måste`.
3. Add the object with `en` or `ett`.
4. Check: verb second, `inte` after verb, question words first.

## Quick reminder

- Statement: `Hon äter ett äpple.`
- Helper: `Jag vill äta.`
- Yes/no: `Har du en bil?`
- Question: `Var bor du?`
- Not: `Jag äter inte.`
- Time first: `Idag äter jag en smörgås.`

## How the exercise works

Tap the Swedish word tiles in the right order. One tile is extra — tap a word in your answer to take it back.
