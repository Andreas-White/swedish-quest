# Small talk

Swedes greet often and briefly. Learn these six moves and you can open and close any chat.

- `Hej hej!` (Hello hello — a friendly hello.)
- `Hur mår du?` (How are you?)
- `Tack bra.` (Thanks, fine.)
- `Ursäkta.` (Sorry / excuse me.)
- `Varsågod.` (Here you go / you are welcome.)
- `Hej då!` (Goodbye!)

## Hello and how are you

`Hej` works everywhere. Doubling it sounds warmer.

- `Hej!` (Hello!)
- `Hej hej!` (Hi hi!)

Then ask:

- `Hur mår du?` (How are you? — one person.)
- `Tack bra.` (Thanks, I am fine.)
- `Tack bra, och du?` (Thanks fine, and you?)

`Tack` means thanks. It appears in many polite answers.

## Sorry, please, you are welcome

- `Ursäkta.` (Sorry / excuse me — bumping, asking a question.)
- `Förlåt.` (Sorry — apologising for a mistake.)
- `Tack.` (Thanks.)
- `Varsågod.` (Here you go — handing something over. Also "you are welcome".)

A café exchange:

- `En kaffe, tack.` (One coffee, please.)
- `Varsågod.` (Here you go.)

## Goodbye

- `Hej då!` (Goodbye!)
- `Vi ses!` (See you!)
- `Hej så länge!` (Bye for now!)

`Vi ses` literally means "we see each other". Use it when you expect to meet again.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ `Hur må du?` → ✓ `Hur mår du?` (The verb is `mår`.)
- ✗ `Tack då!` → ✓ `Hej då!` (`Tack` is thanks, `hej då` is goodbye.)
- ✗ `Vi se!` → ✓ `Vi ses!` (It ends with `ses`.)

## Summary

- Greet with `Hej hej!`
- Ask with `Hur mår du?`
- Answer with `Tack bra.`
- Soften with `Ursäkta.`
- Leave with `Hej då!` or `Vi ses!`
