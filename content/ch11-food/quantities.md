# Quantities

Shopping needs amounts: `en liter`, `ett kilo`, `ett paket`, plus `mycket` (much) and `lite` (a little).

## Fixed amounts

- `en liter mjölk` (one litre of milk)
- `ett kilo äpplen` (one kilo of apples)
- `ett paket kaffe` (one packet of coffee)
- `en burk sylt` (one jar of jam)
- `Jag vill ha ett kilo äpplen.` (I would like a kilo of apples.)

## Mycket and lite

- `mycket mjölk` (much milk / a lot of milk)
- `lite mjölk` (a little milk)
- `Hur mycket mjölk vill du ha?` (How much milk do you want?)
- `Bara lite, tack.` (Just a little, please.)
- `Inte så mycket.` (Not so much.)

## Asking how much

- `Hur mycket kostar brödet?` (How much does the bread cost?)
- `Hur många äpplen vill du ha?` (How many apples do you want?)
- `Två kilo, tack.` (Two kilos, please.)
- `Det räcker så.` (That is enough.)

Use `hur mycket` for uncountable (milk, cheese) and `hur många` for countable (apples).

## Common mistakes

- ✗ `ett liter mjölk` → ✓ `en liter mjölk.` (Liter is en-word: en liter.)
- ✗ `Hur många mjölk?` → ✓ `Hur mycket mjölk?` (Milk is uncountable: hur mycket.)
- ✗ `Jag vill ha lite många äpplen.` → ✓ `Jag vill ha några äpplen.` or `Jag vill ha lite frukt.` (Do not mix lite + många.)

## Summary

- `en liter mjölk`, `ett kilo äpplen`, `ett paket kaffe`.
- `mycket` = much, `lite` = a little.
- Ask `Hur mycket…?` or `Hur många…?`
