# Time prepositions

Swedish time words each need their own preposition: `på` for days, `i` for months, `klockan` for clock time, `om` for in/for.

## På, i, klockan

- `på måndag` (on Monday)
- `på fredag` (on Friday)
- `i juni` (in June)
- `i december` (in December)
- `klockan tre` (at three o'clock)

Sentences: `Vi ses på måndag.` (We meet on Monday.) `Hon kommer i juni.` (She comes in June.) `Jag stiger upp klockan sju.` (I get up at seven.)

## Om: in / for

`Om` means in/for with future time: `om en timme` (in an hour), `om en vecka` (in a week).

- `Vi ses om en timme.` (We meet in an hour.)
- `Han kommer om en vecka.` (He comes in a week.)
- `Bussen går om tio minuter.` (The bus leaves in ten minutes.)
- `Vi reser om en månad.` (We travel in a month.)

## Common mistakes

- ✗ `i måndag` → ✓ `på måndag` (Days take på.)
- ✗ `på juni` → ✓ `i juni` (Months take i.)
- ✗ `på klockan tre` → ✓ `klockan tre` (No på before klockan.)

## Summary

- `på måndag` for days, `i juni` for months.
- `klockan tre` for clock time, no preposition before klockan.
- `om en timme` = in an hour.
