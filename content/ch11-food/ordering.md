# Ordering food and drink

One polite phrase opens every order: `Jag skulle vilja ha…` (I would like to have…).

## The polite order

- `Jag skulle vilja ha kaffe.` (I would like coffee.)
- `Jag skulle vilja ha en smörgås.` (I would like a sandwich.)
- `En kanelbulle, tack.` (A cinnamon bun, please.)
- `Ett glas vatten, tack.` (A glass of water, please.)

## At the table

- `Får jag se menyn?` (May I see the menu?)
- `Vad rekommenderar du?` (What do you recommend?)
- `Det smakar gott.` (It tastes good.)
- `Notan, tack.` (The bill, please.)

## Paying

- `Kan jag betala med kort?` (Can I pay by card?)
- `Ja, det går bra.` (Yes, that works.)
- `Tack! Hej då!` (Thanks! Bye!)

## Common mistakes

- ✗ `Jag vill kaffe nu.` → ✓ `Jag skulle vilja ha kaffe.` (Skulle vilja ha is the polite form.)
- ✗ `Nota tack.` → ✓ `Notan, tack.` (The bill is bestämd form: notan.)
- ✗ `Jag skulle vilja har kaffe.` → ✓ `Jag skulle vilja ha kaffe.` (After vilja, use ha, not har.)

## Summary

- Order with `Jag skulle vilja ha…`
- `Får jag se menyn?` `Det smakar gott.`
- Pay with `Notan, tack.`
