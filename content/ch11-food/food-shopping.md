# Food shopping

A short food vocabulary plus one key sentence covers most shopping trips.

## Common foods

- `bröd` (bread)
- `mjölk` (milk)
- `ost` (cheese)
- `kött` (meat)
- `fisk` (fish)
- `frukt` (fruit)
- `äpple` (apple)
- `kaffe` (coffee)

## At the shop

The key sentence is `Jag handlar mat.` (I am grocery shopping.)

- `Jag handlar mat.` (I buy food / I grocery-shop.)
- `Jag köper bröd.` (I buy bread.)
- `Jag köper mjölk och ost.` (I buy milk and cheese.)
- `Var finns frukten?` (Where is the fruit?)
- `Hur mycket kostar fisken?` (How much does the fish cost?)

## Quantities in the basket

- `en liter mjölk` (one litre of milk)
- `ett kilo äpplen` (one kilo of apples)
- `Jag vill ha bröd.` (I would like bread.)

## Common mistakes

- ✗ `Jag handlar köket.` → ✓ `Jag handlar mat.` (Handla + mat means grocery-shop.)
- ✗ `Jag köper mjölken och ost.` → ✓ `Jag köper mjölk och ost.` (No definite form when you mean some milk and cheese.)
- ✗ `Hur mycket kostar?` → ✓ `Hur mycket kostar fisken?` (Name what you ask the price of.)

## Summary

- Foods: `bröd`, `mjölk`, `ost`, `kött`, `fisk`, `frukt`.
- `Jag handlar mat.` `Jag köper bröd.`
- Ask `Hur mycket kostar fisken?`
