# Adding an object: en and ett

Like English, the object comes after the verb: `Jag läser en bok.`

## Two kinds of nouns

Swedish has two words for “a”: `en` and `ett`. Learn the article with the noun.

- en-words: `en bok`, `en bil`, `en flicka`, `en tidning`, `en smörgås`
- ett-words: `ett hus`, `ett äpple`, `ett barn`

Most nouns are en-words — `en` is the safer guess.

## Wanting things: vill ha

English “He wants a sandwich” = Swedish “He wants *to have* a sandwich”.

- `Han vill ha en smörgås.`

## With helper verbs

Subject, helper verb, infinitive, then the object: `Jag vill läsa en bok.`

## Common mistakes

- ✗ `ett bok` → ✓ `en bok.`
- ✗ `Han vill en smörgås.` → ✓ `Han vill ha en smörgås.`
- ✗ `Jag en bok läser.` → ✓ `Jag läser en bok.`
