# Café dialogue

Here is a full fika order — a `kanelbulle` (cinnamon bun) plus coffee — with phrases you can reuse.

## The dialogue

- Staff: `Hej! Vad får det vara?` (Hi! What would you like?)
- You: `Jag skulle vilja ha kaffe.` (I would like coffee.)
- You: `Och en kanelbulle, tack.` (And a cinnamon bun, please.)
- Staff: `Att äta här eller ta med?` (To eat here or take away?)
- You: `Att äta här, tack.` (To eat here, please.)
- Staff: `Det blir femtio kronor.` (That is fifty kronor.)
- You: `Kan jag betala med kort?` (Can I pay by card?)
- Staff: `Ja, det går bra.` (Yes, that works.)
- You: `Tack! Hej då!` (Thanks! Bye!)

## Role-play phrases

- `Vad får det vara?` (What would you like? — staff to you)
- `Jag skulle vilja ha te.` (I would like tea.)
- `En kanelbulle till, tack.` (One more cinnamon bun, please.)
- `Att ta med, tack.` (Takeaway, please.)
- `Notan, tack.` (The bill, please.)

## Common mistakes

- ✗ `Jag vill ha kaffe nu tack nu.` → ✓ `Jag skulle vilja ha kaffe.` (Polite and short is best.)
- ✗ `Äta här eller ta med eller här?` → ✓ `Att äta här, tack.` (Pick one: här or ta med.)
- ✗ `Kan jag betala med kontanter kort?` → ✓ `Kan jag betala med kort?` (Name one payment method.)

## Summary

- Greet, order, choose `här` or `ta med`, pay, say thanks.
- Core order: `Jag skulle vilja ha kaffe.` `En kanelbulle, tack.`
- Pay: `Kan jag betala med kort?`
