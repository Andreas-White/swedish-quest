# Greetings and introductions

Use these from day one:

- `Jag heter Maria.` (My name is Maria.)
- `Jag är från …` (I am from …)
- `Jag bor i Sverige.` (I live in Sweden.)
- `Hej!` `Tack!` `Hej då!`

`Heter` comes from `heta` — “to be called”. One form for everyone.

Since the 1960s, Swedes say `du` (you) to almost everyone — teachers and bosses included.
