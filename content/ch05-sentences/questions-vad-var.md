# Questions with vad and var

Question words start the sentence, then the verb comes second and the subject third. The pattern is **question word + verb + subject**.

- `Vad äter du?` (What are you eating?)
- `Var bor du?` (Where do you live?)

## The six question words

- `vad` = what
- `var` = where
- `vem` = who
- `när` = when
- `varför` = why
- `hur` = how

## Question word + verb + subject

The verb always follows the question word directly. The subject comes after the verb.

- `Vad läser du?`
- `Var bor hon?`
- `Vem är han?`
- `När kommer du?`
- `Varför skrattar du?`
- `Hur mår du?`

Note `Hur mår du` is the standard way to ask how someone is.

## Answering simply

Answer with a normal statement: subject first, verb second.

- `Var bor du?` → `Jag bor här.`
- `Vad läser du?` → `Jag läser en bok.`
- `När kommer han?` → `Han kommer imorgon.`

## Common mistakes

- ✗ `Vad du äter?` → ✓ `Vad äter du?` (The verb comes right after the question word.)
- ✗ `Var du bor?` → ✓ `Var bor du?` (Question word + verb + subject.)
- ✗ `Vem du är?` → ✓ `Vem är du?` (Never put the subject before the verb here.)

## Summary

- Six key words: `vad`, `var`, `vem`, `när`, `varför`, `hur`.
- Order is always question word + verb + subject.
- `Var bor du?` and `Vad äter du?` show the pattern.
- Answers use normal order: `Jag bor här.`
