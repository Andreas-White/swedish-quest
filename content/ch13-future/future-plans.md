# Future plans with ska

Swedish has no future ending on the verb. For plans and decisions, use `ska` plus the infinitive.

- `Imorgon ska jag resa.`
- `Vi ska laga mat.`
- `Han ska ringa dig.`

## Ska + infinitive

Put `ska` right after the subject. The main verb stays in the infinitive.

- `Jag ska arbeta.`
- `Du ska sova tidigt.`
- `De ska köpa biljetter.`
- `Hon ska stanna hemma.`

## Time words first

Time words often start the sentence. Then `ska` comes before the subject.

- `Imorgon ska jag arbeta.`
- `Ikväll ska vi laga mat.`
- `På fredag ska hon resa.`

## Questions and negatives

Questions start with `ska`. Negatives add `inte` after `ska`.

- `Ska du komma imorgon?`
- `Ska ni stanna hemma?`
- `Jag ska inte arbeta ikväll.`
- `Vi ska inte resa imorgon.`

## Common mistakes

- ✗ `Jag ska äter.` → ✓ `Jag ska äta.` (After `ska`, use the infinitive.)
- ✗ `Imorgon jag ska resa.` → ✓ `Imorgon ska jag resa.` (Time first means verb second.)
- ✗ `Jag ska inte äter.` → ✓ `Jag ska inte äta.` (`Inte` does not change the infinitive.)

## Summary

- Use `ska` + infinitive for plans: `Vi ska resa.`
- Time words like `Imorgon` and `Ikväll` often start the sentence.
- After a fronted time word, `ska` comes before the subject.
